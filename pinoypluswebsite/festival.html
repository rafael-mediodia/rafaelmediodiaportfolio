What to expect

Designer talks

Filipino creatives share how identity, work, and practice show up in their studios — followed by open Q&A.

Open studios

Walk through booths, see work in progress, and meet artists across graphic design, fashion, photo, and more.

Food & community

Lumpia, rice plates, and whatever people bring. Non-Filipinos welcome — that's the whole point of plus one.

Program

Kasama showcase

Rotating vinyl across the gallery spotlights work from Filipino creatives in our network. The festival brings that work into the room; their profiles live here year-round for you to browse anytime.
