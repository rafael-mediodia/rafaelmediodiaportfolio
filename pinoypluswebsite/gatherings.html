Gatherings

Let's celebrate.

Smaller meetups throughout the year — end-of-season potlucks, portfolio reviews, and the occasional karaoke night. Usually twenty to thirty people. Always BYO dish.

How it works

Gatherings are informal and free. We send dates through our community list and post them here. No dress code, no presentation required — show up with food to share and something you've been working on if you want feedback.

  • Portfolio reviews — bring prints or a laptop
  • Roundtable discussions — seasonal themes, open floor
  • Karaoke — optional, always encouraged
