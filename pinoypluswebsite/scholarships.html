Scholarships

Support for emerging makers.

Two small grants each fall for Filipino students and early-career artists. Covers materials, residency fees, or a month of studio rent.

Who can apply

    What to send

    A short proposal (500 words max), five images or links to recent work, and a line on how you'd use the grant. We review blind — names are hidden during the first pass.

    Apply by email →

    Questions

    Can I apply if I'm not in the Bay Area?
    Yes — we support makers across the diaspora. Funds are sent in USD; international recipients handle conversion on their end.
    Is this tied to the festival?
    No obligation to attend, but recipients are invited to share work at a gathering or the following year's festival if they'd like.
    When do recipients hear back?
    Within six weeks of the October 15 deadline. Two recipients announced each November.
