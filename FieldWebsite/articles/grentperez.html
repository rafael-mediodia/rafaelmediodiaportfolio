grentperez and his current listens

Soft rock, Filipino ballads, and the gut feelings behind a genre-fluid debut.

The Sydney-born artist invites us into a headspace shaped by 70s soft rock, video game soundtracks, and a deep-seated love for Filipino ballads. Grentperez explains how these "different flavors" informed the genre-fluidity of his debut album, Backflips in a Restaurant, a record that drifts between bedroom pop confessionals and sun-drenched soul without ever feeling like it's trying too hard.

From his distaste for Kiki's Delivery Service to the "gut feelings" that drive his songwriting, our interview captures a young creator navigating his 20s with a rare balance of playful curiosity and creative discipline. Here's what he has on repeat right now:

Current listens:

  • Steely Dan and "Peg" (the 70s soft rock gateway drug)
  • Nintendo soundtracks, especially the Animal Crossing OST on loop while writing
  • Regine Velasquez, Filipino ballad royalty, always
  • Mk.gee and "Alesis" (a fellow traveler in the quiet lane)
