the geometry of quiet: mk.gee

Minimal textures placed like architecture, with each silence given equal weight.

Mk.gee's recent studio sessions feel like architecture, each sound placed with the precision of a draftsman and each silence given equal weight. We trace the minimal, ambient textures that have made his work a touchstone for a generation of bedroom producers reaching for something more spacious.

"I'm not trying to fill the room. I'm trying to figure out what shape the room already is."
