seeing the light with orion sun

Private memories translated into the lush, golden hour atmosphere that defines her sound.

In our latest deep dive, the multifaceted artist discusses the delicate process of translating private memories into the lush, atmospheric arrangements that define her signature sound. Orion Sun has always made music that feels like it was recorded at golden hour, warm and hazy and impossibly intimate.

She explores how her transition to a more communal life in Los Angeles has reshaped her creative solitude. Where her earlier work was born in the quiet of a Philadelphia bedroom, her new material channels the strange openness of the West Coast, with wide skies, new collaborators, and the vulnerability of being seen.

"I used to think solitude was the only way to be honest. Now I think honesty is what happens when you're not alone anymore and you let people into the process and trust that the feeling survives."
